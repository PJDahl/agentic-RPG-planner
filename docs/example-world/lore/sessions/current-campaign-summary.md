# Current Campaign Summary

## Summary
The campaign begins as ash storms worsen around Ashgate. The players are drawn into a conflict between the Eldwatch, the Ashen Temple, the Emberbound, and noble houses over the failing Ashen Compact.

## Player Discoveries
By default, the players may know:
- Ash storms are increasing.
- Poor districts suffer more than noble districts.
- The Ashen Temple claims the Compact is stable.
- The Emberbound claim the Compact is a lie.

## Secrets Still Hidden
- Caldus altered ward reports.
- Isolde has stolen temple records.
- Nyra dreams of the Ashen King.
- The Ashen King is speaking through cracks in the Compact.
- The first Compact involved an erased sacrifice.

## Suggested Opening Hook
The players witness a ward failure in the Cindervein. A flame burns cold blue and whispers one of their names.
