# Stolen Temple Records

## Mystery Summary
Someone has stolen partial Ashen Temple records showing falsified ward readings. Lady Isolde Varric currently possesses copies.

## Truth
Isolde bought the records through intermediaries and is waiting to reveal them at the politically ideal moment.

## Public Belief
No public scandal exists yet.

## What Players Might Discover
- A dead clerk had temple ash under their fingernails.
- Isolde's servants know too much about ward failures.
- Caldus is searching for a missing ledger.

## Consequences of Revelation
- Caldus becomes vulnerable.
- Isolde may pretend to be a reformer.
- Selene may demand full temple access.
- The Emberbound may riot.
