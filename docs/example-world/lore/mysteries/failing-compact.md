# The Failing Compact

## Mystery Summary
The Ashen Compact that protects Ashgate is failing. The public does not know, and powerful figures are hiding different parts of the truth.

## Truth
The Compact is weakening because the deeper seals beneath the Hearthseal are cracking. The surface rituals still work, but less reliably each year.

## Public Belief
The Compact remains sacred and stable as long as citizens keep faith and the temple maintains the Hearthfire.

## What Players Might Know
- Ash storms are increasing.
- Poor districts suffer more failures.
- Temple officials seem evasive.

## What Players Do Not Know
- Caldus altered ward reports.
- The Ashen King has begun speaking through flames and dreams.
- The first Compact may have required a hidden sacrifice.
- Breaking the Compact could be catastrophic or liberating.

## Clues Already Revealed
- None by default.

## Future Clues
- Contradictory ward readings.
- A flame that speaks a player's name.
- Temple records with scraped-away numbers.
- Ash-glass shipments diverted to noble districts.
- An old Compact hymn with missing verses.

## Consequences of Revelation
- Public panic.
- Emberbound uprising.
- Temple schism.
- Selene forced into emergency rule.
- Isolde tries to control the replacement order.
