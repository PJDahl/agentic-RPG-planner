# The Emberbound

## Summary
The Emberbound are rebels, mystics, workers, and arsonists united by the belief that the Ashen Compact is a lie that protects the powerful by sacrificing the powerless.

## Public Reputation
The ruling class calls them terrorists. Poor districts often see them as dangerous truth-tellers.

## Goals
- Reveal the truth of the Compact.
- Break temple control over Ashgate.
- Stop nobles from profiting from ward failures.
- Free, renegotiate with, or expose the Ashen King.

## Knows
- Poor districts suffer more ward failures.
- Temple reports are inconsistent.
- Foundry workers have seen ash-glass shipments diverted.
- Nyra receives meaningful dreams.

## Does Not Know
- Isolde has manipulated some information channels.
- The full cost of breaking the Compact.
- Whether the Ashen King is honest.
- Selene has emergency plans to protect poor districts.

## Internal Divisions
- Nyra's liberationists.
- Hardline arson cells.
- Workers seeking reforms.
- Mystics who worship the Ashen King.
- Opportunists who simply want revenge.

## Resources
- Street networks.
- Hidden safehouses.
- Foundry contacts.
- Smuggled ash-glass explosives.
- Public anger.

## Secrets
- Some cells are willing to burn inhabited noble buildings.
- Some members worship the Ashen King outright.
