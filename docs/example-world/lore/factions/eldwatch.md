# The Eldwatch

## Summary
The Eldwatch is Ashgate's civic and military defense order. It maintains shelters, patrols ash-storm borders, fights fire-warped beasts, and enforces emergency law.

## Goals
- Protect civilians.
- Maintain order.
- Investigate ward failures.
- Prevent civil conflict.
- Keep Ashgate functioning during ash storms.

## Knows
- Ward failures are increasing.
- The temple is not sharing everything.
- The Emberbound have infiltrated several districts.
- Noble houses are stockpiling private guards.

## Does Not Know
- The full truth of Caldus's cover-up.
- The Ashen King's full influence.
- Which nobles have stolen records.
- Whether the Compact can be renewed.

## Internal Divisions
- Selene loyalists.
- Hardliners who want martial law.
- Ward engineers focused on practical repairs.
- Street wardens sympathetic to the poor.

## Resources
- Soldiers.
- Investigators.
- Shelters.
- Ward engineers.
- Civic authority.
