# The Ashen Temple

## Summary
The Ashen Temple preserves the rituals of the Ashen Compact and teaches that sacrifice, discipline, and devotion keep Eldmere safe from the Cinderfall's return.

## Public Beliefs
- The Compact is sacred.
- The Ashen King was defeated and bound for the good of all.
- The Hearthfire must never go out.
- Doubt weakens the wards.

## Secret Truth
Senior temple figures know the Compact is weakening and that the public ritual history is incomplete.

## Goals
- Preserve faith.
- Maintain the Hearthfire.
- Prevent panic.
- Keep forbidden Compact lore restricted.
- Stop the Emberbound from spreading heresy.

## Knows
- Ward failures are increasing.
- Caldus has hidden some records, though not every priest knows this.
- The Emberbound are searching for proof.
- Public faith matters to civic stability.

## Does Not Know
- The Ashen King's full current influence.
- How many temple records Isolde has stolen.
- Whether Caldus is considering forbidden rites.
- Whether the original Compact can be safely renewed.

## Internal Divisions
- Compassionate priests who want transparency.
- Hardliners who want dissent crushed.
- Ritualists who care more about procedure than truth.
- Caldus loyalists.

## Secrets
- Some records were altered.
- The sacred history omits the human cost of the first Compact.
