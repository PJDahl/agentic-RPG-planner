# Nyra of the Cinders

## Summary
Nyra is the most visible leader of the Emberbound, a revolutionary group that believes the Ashen Compact is a lie built on sacrifice. She survived a childhood ash storm that killed her family while noble districts remained protected.

## Current Status
Nyra is receiving dreams from a burning crowned figure. She believes the dreams prove the Compact imprisons a wronged spirit, but she does not know whether the spirit is honest.

## Core Motivation
Nyra wants to break the system that protects the powerful while sacrificing the poor.

## Personality
- Fierce
- Charismatic
- Angry
- Protective of commoners
- Suspicious of compromise
- Capable of mercy
- Drawn toward dangerous symbols

## Knows
- The wards fail more often in poor districts.
- The temple lies about ash storm casualties.
- The Compact is not what the public has been told.
- The Emberbound have infiltrators in foundries and lower temple offices.
- Some dreams contain information she should not otherwise know.

## Does Not Know
- Whether the Ashen King is manipulating her.
- The full cost of breaking the Compact.
- That Isolde may be feeding information to radicals through intermediaries.
- Caldus's full role in the cover-up.
- Selene's evacuation plans.

## Resources
- Emberbound cells.
- Street support in poor districts.
- Hidden safehouses.
- Symbolic authority among anti-Compact rebels.
- Information from dreams.

## Constraints
- The movement contains violent extremists.
- Nobles can frame her for disasters.
- Selene can crack down if civilians are endangered.
- Nyra's dreams may be unreliable.
- If she hesitates, harder radicals may replace her.

## Relationships

### Selene Marrow
Nyra sees Selene as the most honorable face of a rotten order.

### Brother Caldus Vey
Nyra sees Caldus as a liar preserving a murderous myth.

### Lady Isolde Varric
Nyra despises Isolde but may unknowingly use information Isolde leaked.

## Secrets
- Nyra has spoken with the Ashen King in dreams.
- She is unsure whether she wants to free him or force the truth into the open.
- She fears she is becoming a symbol she cannot control.

## Open Questions
- Would Nyra accept a repaired Compact if the truth were public?
- Can she keep the Emberbound from becoming indiscriminate arsonists?
