# High Warden Selene Marrow

## Summary
Selene Marrow commands the Eldwatch, the military and civic order that protects Eldmere from ash storms, fire-warped beasts, and civil unrest. She is not queen, but in Ashgate her authority often matters more than the crown's distant decrees.

## Current Status
Selene is investigating a rise in ash storms and ward failures around Ashgate. She suspects someone is tampering with the Compact but lacks proof.

## Core Motivation
Selene wants Eldmere to survive without becoming a police state or a temple-ruled theocracy.

## Personality
- Disciplined
- Protective
- Suspicious
- Practical
- Tired of lies but aware truth can kill
- More compassionate than she appears

## Knows
- The ash wards are weakening.
- The Ashen Temple has concealed some ward readings.
- Lady Isolde is gathering political leverage.
- The Emberbound have grown bolder.
- The Ashen Compact exists and binds a fire spirit beneath Ashgate.
- The public version of the Compact is incomplete.

## Does Not Know
- Brother Caldus has personally hidden evidence that the Compact is failing.
- The Ashen King is speaking to multiple mortals through dreams.
- Lady Isolde has a private copy of several temple records.
- Whether breaking the Compact would free, kill, or empower the Ashen King.

## Resources
- Eldwatch soldiers and investigators.
- Legal authority in Ashgate.
- Ward engineers.
- Emergency shelters.
- Access to civic archives.

## Constraints
- Public panic could kill thousands.
- The Ashen Temple is politically powerful.
- Nobles may block funding or troops.
- She cannot arrest priests without evidence.
- She cannot repair the Compact alone.

## Relationships

### Brother Caldus Vey
Selene sees Caldus as evasive but not necessarily corrupt. She suspects he knows more than he admits.

### Lady Isolde Varric
Selene distrusts Isolde's opportunism but may need her resources.

### Nyra of the Cinders
Selene considers Nyra dangerous, but understands why desperate people follow her.

## Secrets
- Selene knows more about the Compact than most citizens.
- She has contingency plans to evacuate Ashgate districts if the wards fail.

## Open Questions
- How far will Selene go if public order collapses?
- Does she have a trusted heir or second-in-command?
