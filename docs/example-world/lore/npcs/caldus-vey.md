# Brother Caldus Vey

## Summary
Brother Caldus Vey is the Keeper of the Hearth, a senior priest of the Ashen Temple. Publicly, he teaches that the Ashen Compact is a sacred covenant that protects Eldmere. Privately, he knows the Compact is failing.

## Current Status
Caldus has hidden several ward-readings and altered temple reports to prevent panic. He is searching for a way to renew the Compact before anyone exposes the truth.

## Core Motivation
Caldus wants to preserve faith and prevent mass panic, even if doing so requires lies.

## Personality
- Gentle in public
- Fearful in private
- Intelligent
- Paternalistic
- Skilled at moral rationalization
- Genuinely cares about the people

## Knows
- The Compact is failing faster than the temple admits.
- Some ash storms are not natural.
- The Ashen King has begun whispering through sacred flames.
- The temple hierarchy would fracture if the truth became public.
- Selene suspects concealment.
- Lady Isolde may possess stolen temple records.

## Does Not Know
- The Ashen King's full goal.
- Whether renewing the Compact will require a living sacrifice.
- Whether Nyra's dreams are from the Ashen King or another source.
- How much the players know.
- Whether Selene would protect him or arrest him if she learned the truth.

## Resources
- Temple records.
- Loyal acolytes.
- Public trust.
- Ritual access to the central Hearthfire.
- Authority over religious messaging.

## Constraints
- His lies are becoming harder to maintain.
- Temple records can expose him.
- The Emberbound are looking for proof of corruption.
- He cannot personally repair the Compact without forbidden rites.

## Relationships

### Selene Marrow
Caldus respects Selene but fears her directness. He believes she would mishandle the truth by moving too forcefully.

### Lady Isolde Varric
Caldus fears Isolde will weaponize the truth for political gain.

### Nyra of the Cinders
Caldus views Nyra as proof that uncontrolled truth becomes violence.

## Secrets
- Caldus altered ward reports.
- Caldus has heard the Ashen King speak.
- Caldus is considering a forbidden renewal rite.

## Open Questions
- What price would Caldus pay to renew the Compact?
- Has he already performed a smaller forbidden rite?
