# Lady Isolde Varric

## Summary
Lady Isolde Varric is the head of one of Ashgate's wealthiest noble houses. Her family owns ash-glass foundries, caravan routes, and several ward-maintenance contracts.

## Current Status
Isolde has acquired partial temple records showing that the Compact is failing. She has not revealed them because the timing is not yet useful.

## Core Motivation
Isolde wants House Varric to control whatever replaces the current order.

## Personality
- Elegant
- Ruthless
- Patient
- Politically brilliant
- Publicly charitable
- Privately calculating
- Prefers blackmail to bloodshed

## Knows
- The Ashen Temple has hidden dangerous ward readings.
- Caldus is personally involved in the cover-up.
- Selene is investigating the same issue.
- The Emberbound can be useful if pointed at the right target.
- The Compact is politically more important than spiritually understood.

## Does Not Know
- The full nature of the Ashen King.
- Whether the Compact can be renewed.
- That the Ashen King is speaking to multiple people.
- The full content of Caldus's forbidden rite notes.
- Whether the players can be bought, threatened, or turned into public heroes.

## Resources
- Wealth.
- Spies.
- Foundry workers.
- Noble influence.
- Stolen temple records.
- Bribed clerks and ward engineers.

## Constraints
- If blamed for destabilizing the city, her house could fall.
- Selene watches her closely.
- The Emberbound hate nobles.
- The temple can turn public faith against her.
- Her own contracts implicate her in ward neglect.

## Relationships

### Selene Marrow
Isolde sees Selene as admirable but politically naive.

### Brother Caldus Vey
Isolde sees Caldus as a useful hypocrite.

### Nyra of the Cinders
Isolde sees Nyra as a dangerous spark that can be aimed once, but not controlled twice.

## Secrets
- Isolde has stolen temple records.
- House Varric profited from cheap ward materials.
- Isolde may prefer a controlled crisis over a quiet repair.

## Open Questions
- Would Isolde betray the city to preserve her house?
- Does she have a genuine affection for anyone in Ashgate?
