# Mara Vel

## Summary
Mara Vel is a Cindervein-born rogue who steals from noble couriers and sells information to survive. She recently intercepted a sealed temple ledger without realizing it is connected to the failing Ashen Compact.

## Player-Facing Identity
Mara is a sharp-tongued street thief from the Cindervein who distrusts nobles, priests, and wardens. She presents herself as selfish, but she quietly protects families in her old neighborhood.

## Private / DM-Only Backstory
The ledger Mara stole is one of the missing temple records Lady Isolde Varric has been searching for. It contains partial evidence that Brother Caldus altered ward readings.

## Current Status
Mara is deciding whether to sell the ledger, hide it, or use it to protect the Cindervein. She does not yet understand how dangerous the information is.

## Ancestry / Class / Background
- **Ancestry:** Human
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Background:** Urchin / Criminal
- **Level:** 1
- **Alignment / Outlook:** Suspicious but protective

## Core Motivation
Mara wants enough leverage that no noble, priest, or warden can decide the fate of her district without consequences.

## Secondary Goals
- Protect her younger brother from the Emberbound hardliners.
- Find out why the Cindervein wards fail so often.
- Humiliate House Varric if she can do so safely.

## Personality
- Sarcastic
- Observant
- Defensive
- Secretly loyal

## Ideals
- People in power only listen when they are afraid.
- The poor deserve truth, not sermons.

## Bonds
- Her younger brother still lives in the Cindervein.
- She owes an old favor to an Eldwatch street warden.

## Flaws
- She assumes every authority figure is lying.
- She risks too much when someone from the Cindervein is threatened.

## Fears
- Becoming a pawn in noble politics.
- Learning that the city cannot be saved.

## Knows
- The Cindervein wards fail more often than noble district wards.
- The Emberbound are recruiting aggressively.
- The stolen ledger is valuable.
- House Varric agents are looking for missing documents.

## Does Not Know
- The ledger implicates Brother Caldus.
- Lady Isolde already has other stolen records.
- The Ashen King is speaking through the weakening Compact.
- Releasing the ledger publicly could trigger riots.

## Player Knows
- Mara stole a temple ledger.
- The ledger is probably dangerous.
- Someone wealthy wants it back.

## Other Players Know
- Mara is from the Cindervein.
- Mara distrusts the Ashen Temple and House Varric.

## Resources
- Stolen temple ledger.
- Contacts among Cindervein street children.
- A hidden route through old ash-glass drains.
- Lockpicks and forged courier marks.

## Constraints
- House Varric agents may recognize her.
- Emberbound hardliners may try to recruit or pressure her.
- She lacks the magical knowledge to understand the ledger.
- She struggles to trust allies with sensitive information.

## Relationships

### Lady Isolde Varric
Mara sees Isolde as everything wrong with Ashgate’s nobility, though she does not yet know Isolde also wants the stolen records.

### Nyra of the Cinders
Mara admires Nyra’s anger but fears the Emberbound will get people in the Cindervein killed.

### High Warden Selene Marrow
Mara distrusts Selene as another face of authority, but may slowly realize Selene genuinely wants to protect civilians.

## Personal Arc
Mara’s arc is about learning the difference between leverage, truth, and trust. She begins by treating secrets as weapons, but may become someone who decides when truth must be shared even at personal cost.

## Arc Questions
- Will Mara trust anyone in power with the ledger?
- Can she protect the Cindervein without empowering extremists?
- Does she become a revolutionary, informant, hero, or blackmailer?

## Secrets
- The ledger is part of the evidence proving the Compact is failing.
- Isolde may frame Mara as an Emberbound thief if the scandal breaks badly.
- The ledger contains a name connected to the original Compact sacrifice.

## Clues to Reveal
- A ward engineer recognizes the numbers in the ledger.
- A temple acolyte panics when they see the seal on the first page.
- Isolde’s agents offer far too much money for “a worthless old book.”
- The Ashen King whispers a phrase from the ledger through a candle flame.

## Possible Future Developments
- Mara gives the ledger to Selene, triggering a quiet investigation.
- Mara gives the ledger to Nyra, triggering public unrest.
- Mara sells the ledger to Isolde, gaining wealth but empowering a manipulator.
- Mara keeps the ledger and becomes hunted by multiple factions.

## DM Notes
- Use Mara’s arc to test whether the party values truth, stability, or revenge.
- Avoid forcing her to trust authority too quickly.
- Put pressure on her through the Cindervein, not just through abstract politics.

## Open Questions
- Who originally tipped Mara off about the courier route?
- Does her brother sympathize with the Emberbound?
- What name in the ledger connects to the first Compact?
