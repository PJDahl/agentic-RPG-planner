# Eldmere

## Summary
Eldmere is a realm scarred by the Cinderfall, a magical disaster that turned its eastern forests into ashlands and left the capital dependent on the Ashen Compact.

## Geography
- Western riverlands with farms and trade roads.
- Central highlands around Ashgate.
- Eastern ashlands filled with glassed trees, ash storms, and fire-warped beasts.

## Government
Eldmere has a distant monarch, but Ashgate is effectively governed day-to-day by the Eldwatch, noble houses, and the Ashen Temple.

## Current Conflicts
- Failing wards.
- Religious secrecy.
- Noble opportunism.
- Emberbound rebellion.
- Increasing ash storms.

## Adventure Uses
- Political intrigue.
- Disaster survival.
- Religious mystery.
- Ancient pact consequences.
