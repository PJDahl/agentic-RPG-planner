# Ashgate

## Summary
Ashgate is the capital of Eldmere and the city built around the Hearthseal. Its towers are blackened by old ash, its windows are made of amber ash-glass, and its people live under the protection of ancient wards.

## Atmosphere
- Sacred
- Anxious
- Smoky
- Politically tense
- Beautiful in a severe way

## Important Districts
### The Hearthward
Temple district surrounding the sealed Hearthfire.

### The Sootsteps
Poor district where ward failures are common.

### Varric Row
Noble and mercantile district dominated by House Varric.

### The Warden's Ring
Fortified administrative district of the Eldwatch.

## Current Conflicts
- The Ashen Temple hides failing ward readings.
- The Emberbound recruit in the Sootsteps.
- Lady Isolde gathers leverage.
- Selene investigates without causing panic.

## Secrets
- The Hearthseal beneath the city is cracking.
- Certain noble districts receive stronger ward maintenance than poor districts.
