# The Cindervein

## Summary
The Cindervein is a poor industrial district built near old ash-glass channels. Its wards fail often, and its people distrust both temple and nobility.

## Atmosphere
- Soot-black alleys
- Red-glowing foundries
- Rooftop shrines
- Hidden rebel marks
- Bitter jokes about sacred protection

## Current Conflicts
- Emberbound recruitment.
- Eldwatch patrols.
- Ward failures.
- Foundry exploitation.
- Rumors of fire speaking in dreams.

## Secrets
- Some foundries use cheaper ward-grade glass than reported.
- Isolde's agents have leaked selected information here.
