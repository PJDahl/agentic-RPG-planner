# Main Campaign Timeline

## Ancient / Historical
- Two generations ago: The Cinderfall burns the eastern forests.
- Shortly after: The Ashen Compact binds the Ashen King beneath Ashgate.
- The Ashen Temple forms around the maintenance of the Hearthfire.

## Recent Past
- Ward failures increase in the Sootsteps and Cindervein.
- Caldus alters temple records to hide the rate of failure.
- Isolde acquires stolen temple records.
- Nyra begins receiving dreams from the Ashen King.
- Selene opens a quiet investigation.

## Current Situation
- Ash storms are worsening.
- The Emberbound are recruiting.
- The temple is under quiet pressure.
- Noble houses are preparing for crisis while publicly calling for calm.

## Pending Pressures
- A major ward failure in a poor district.
- A temple record scandal.
- A dream from the Ashen King reaching a player character.
- Selene forced to choose between order and truth.
