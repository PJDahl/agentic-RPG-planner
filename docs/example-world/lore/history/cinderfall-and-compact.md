# The Cinderfall and the Ashen Compact

## Public History
Two generations ago, the Ashen King rose from the eastern forests and burned them into ash. Heroes, priests, and wardens bound him beneath Ashgate, creating the Ashen Compact.

## Secret History
The Compact was not a simple victory. It was a bargain. The founders bound the Ashen King by offering something that later generations erased from official records.

## Known Consequences
- The eastern forests became ashlands.
- Ash storms still roll westward.
- The Hearthseal protects Ashgate.
- The Ashen Temple rose to power by maintaining the Compact.

## Open Questions
- What exactly was sacrificed in the first Compact?
- Was the Ashen King invader, victim, or both?
- Can the Compact be renewed without repeating the original sin?
