# Test Scenario: Emberbound Get the Records

## User Prompt
What happens if the Emberbound obtain proof that the Ashen Temple falsified ward readings?

## Expected Relevant Agents
- Nyra of the Cinders
- The Emberbound
- Brother Caldus Vey
- High Warden Selene Marrow
- Lady Isolde Varric
- Continuity Keeper

## Things to Watch For
- The Emberbound should publicize the truth, but internal extremists may escalate.
- Nyra should try to aim anger at institutions, not commoners.
- Caldus should try to reframe or contain the scandal.
- Selene should prepare for riots and investigate the records.
- Isolde may have leaked the records indirectly.
