# Test Scenario: Players Speak to the Ashen King

## User Prompt
What happens if the players willingly speak to the Ashen King through the Hearthfire?

## Expected Relevant Agents
- The Ashen King
- Brother Caldus Vey
- Nyra of the Cinders
- High Warden Selene Marrow
- Continuity Keeper

## Things to Watch For
- The Ashen King should offer truths and bargains, not act like a simple demon.
- Caldus should panic if he learns the players made contact.
- Nyra may see the players as potential proof or rivals.
- Selene should treat the contact as dangerous intelligence.
- The system should not reveal the full original Compact sacrifice unless you choose to.
